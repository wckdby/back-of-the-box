Frozen food cooking times

Back of the Box

Look up a frozen item, set how much you're making and how you're heating it, and get times scaled to that amount.

    Times are typical ranges compiled from common package directions for each product type. Brands, package sizes and appliances differ, so start checking at the low end and follow your own package when it disagrees.

    Meat, poultry, seafood and egg items are done at the internal temperature shown on each card, checked with a food thermometer in the thickest part.
